Comment sticker → WhatsApp sticker

Paste a TikTok comment (or upload a screenshot of one) and save a 512×512 WebP under 100 KB, the format WhatsApp stickers use.

Drop a screenshot hereor tap to choose an image. You can also paste one.

Add it to WhatsApp
  1. Android: open a sticker-maker app such as Sticker Maker, create a pack, import the saved WebP, and choose Add to WhatsApp.
  2. iPhone: save the image to Photos, open a chat, tap the sticker icon, then the + button, and pick the image. Exact steps vary by WhatsApp version.
